About

I am a Lecturer in Media at City St George’s, University of London. My research sits at the intersection of digital media, the sociology of work and cultural studies, with a particular interest in creator cultures, platforms, food, gender and digital labour.

In particular, I am interested in comparative approaches to understanding how people make sense of, negotiate and resist the social and economic conditions of digital platforms across different contexts. My previous research examined the everyday experiences of women creators on Instagram and Xiaohongshu (Red), exploring how they navigate platform economies, gendered expectations and changing forms of creative and precarious work.

I hold an MPhil and PhD in Sociology from the University of Cambridge.
