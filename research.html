Current Research Projects

Comparative Digital Cultures

This project explores how digital platforms are experienced and negotiated across different social, cultural and political contexts, with particular attention to creator cultures, platform labour and everyday life.

Lying Flat and Everyday Resistance

This project examines forms of everyday resistance in platform economies, focusing on how withdrawal, refusal and alternative ways of living are narrated, sustained and adapted over time.

Eastern Gaze

This project investigates questions of representation, looking and cultural power, with particular attention to how visual and digital cultures are shaped by historically and geopolitically situated ways of seeing.

Counter Research

This project explores alternative and critical approaches to researching global media, including questions of knowledge production, positionality and dominant categories of understanding.
